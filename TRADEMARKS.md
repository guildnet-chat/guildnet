# The GuildNet name and logo

The code is free to use under its [licence](LICENSE). The GuildNet name and
the crest logo aren't: they're trademarks, and the licence doesn't grant
them (section 7 of the AGPL lets us say so). This is so that anything
called GuildNet is GuildNet, and people can trust what they're running.

## Fine, without asking

- Running GuildNet as it comes, for yourself or your company, and calling
  it GuildNet.
- Running it with changes of your own inside your own company, and still
  calling it GuildNet there.
- Saying, truthfully, that your product works with GuildNet, or that it's
  based on GuildNet.
- Writing about GuildNet, teaching it, or running a meetup about it.
- Using the name in links back to this project.

## Ask us first

- Calling a changed version that you give or offer to others GuildNet, or
  anything like it (Guild Net, GuildNet Pro, OpenGuildNet).
- Offering GuildNet as a service to others under the GuildNet name, or in a
  way that suggests it's ours or that we endorse it.
- Using the logo on anything but this project, or a changed logo.
- Putting GuildNet in a product, company or domain name.

## Changed versions

Give yours its own name and look: every page's name, words and logo are
settings ([docs/self-host/brand.md](docs/self-host/brand.md)). Saying
"based on GuildNet" is fine and welcome.
