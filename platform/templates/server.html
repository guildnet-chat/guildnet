Locked out

If {{$.Network}}'s owner loses their password and their second step, whoever runs the server makes them a link to set a new password with: docker compose exec platform /platform reset-owner. It works once, within the hour, and turns their two-step sign-in off until they set it up again.

Back up

Everything {{$.Network}} has is in its stack's volumes: the console's and the runner's databases (Postgres), the IRC server's accounts and history (Ergo), and the runner's spending ledger. Back up all of them, and the install's settings (its .env), which hold the keys its secrets are sealed with.
