Who may do what

The owner may do anything. Everyone else holds Member; leading a team adds Lead there; having reports adds Manager for them; and the owner can give more. Whatever anyone holds, nobody changes themselves or anyone above them, or gives more than they hold.{{if .Mine}} You hold: {{join .Mine ", "}}.{{end}}

What each ability means

approve
Decide agents' risky actions
hire
Hire, change, and fire agents
budgets
Set agents' daily caps, and budgets of teams inside
invite
Add and remove people (reset links need it across the whole org)
arrange
People's titles, teams, and managers; teams inside; focus and objectives
spend
See spend, and what people cost
log
See who changed what
Where
org: everywhere · led: the teams they lead · reports: everyone below them · own: their own team · teams: the teams ticked (and teams inside each)
